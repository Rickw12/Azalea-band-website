About Azalea

Azalea is a sludge metal band that fuses crushing, slow-burning riffs with a deeper, mysterious view of the good in the world. Their lyrics take you to another dimension, where light and darkness converge and the familiar takes on a strange, new shape. With raw vocals, heavy grooves, and an atmospheric undertone, Azalea challenges listeners to experience reality from a different perspective. Brutality and reflection go hand in hand-a sonic storm that both devastates and enlightens.
